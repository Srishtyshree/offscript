## Caveats

- Small test set (110 questions), single run each: a difference of one or two questions is within noise.
- The tuned checkpoint was chosen by validation loss (held-out training rows) before any sealed-set evaluation; no other checkpoint was evaluated on the sealed set.
- The v2 sealed set was written in the same session as the v2 data review, after reading the training data; near-copies were checked (similarity < 0.8).
- Training content was generated by the base model, filtered by rules and corrected in review.
- Content quality beyond these rules (correctness of answers) is not scored here.
